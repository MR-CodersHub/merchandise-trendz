Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 • Last Reviewed: August 28, 2026

At TrendZ Studio ("TrendZ", "we", "our"), protecting the confidential artwork, commercial designs, and personal information of our creators, merchants, and buyers is fundamental to our business. This policy explains how we collect, process, and safeguard your data.

1. Creator Artwork & Design IP Ownership

You retain 100% full intellectual property ownership of all graphics, vector logos, artwork files, and typography uploaded to our mockup studio or print queue.

  • TrendZ will never license, sell, redistribute, or reuse your custom artwork for any purpose other than producing your physical merchandise orders.
  • High-resolution print files are stored in encrypted, air-gapped AWS S3 buckets with 256-bit AES encryption.
  • You may request complete permanent deletion of your artwork files from our print servers at any time.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Data: When registering as a creator or brand, we collect your name, business email, store URL, and payout preference details.

B. Order & Fulfillment Data: When customer orders are placed, we process the recipient shipping address, item sizes, and contact details strictly to generate shipping labels and courier tracking notifications.

C. Payment Information: Financial transactions are processed directly by certified Tier-1 PCI-DSS compliant providers (Stripe & PayPal). TrendZ never stores your credit card numbers or raw bank credentials on our servers.

3. GDPR (Europe) & CCPA (California) Rights

Under global data protection laws, all registered users and customers have explicit rights regarding their personal records:

Right to Access Request a full JSON export of all personal data held about you.
Right to Erasure ("To Be Forgotten") Permanent deletion of your account and customer logs within 30 days.
Right to Rectification Update incorrect shipping records or billing profiles at any time.
Zero Data Selling We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to advertisers.

4. Data Protection Officer Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your GDPR/CCPA rights, contact our Data Privacy Officer:

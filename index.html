Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +
There is strictly 0 Minimum Order Quantity for custom print-on-demand items. You can order just 1 single customized t-shirt, mug, or phone case, or scale up to 10,000+ pieces with bulk wholesale discounts.
What printing methods do you support? +
We offer Direct-to-Garment (DTG), Direct-to-Film (DTF), Dye-Sublimation (for 360° all-over mugs and tumblers), High-Density Screen Printing, Laser Engraving, and 3D Embroidery.
How long does production and shipping take? +
Standard custom orders are printed and dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours. Domestic courier transit takes 1-3 days, and express worldwide shipping takes 3-6 days.
How do creators receive their monthly earnings? +
Creators can request instant payouts directly to bank accounts, PayPal, or Stripe Express. Payouts are processed on a flexible rolling 7-day schedule with full real-time sales visibility.
